What runs on a PC after you install it

One program, Stenwatch.exe, with the .NET runtime inside, so nothing else has to be installed. It opens its own window, shows the report in a WebView2 pane and listens on no port. stenwatch-cli.exe is the same engine without a window, for scheduled runs. Your files live in %APPDATA%\Stenwatch, apart from the program, so updating or uninstalling never touches them unless you say so. The feed download is the only traffic leaving the machine.
