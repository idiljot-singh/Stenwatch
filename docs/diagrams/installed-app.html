What runs on a PC after you install it

Two small programs share one folder. Stenwatch.exe serves the console on 127.0.0.1 only and opens it in an Edge app window; it starts stenwatch-cli.exe for the heavy work so output can stream back. Your files live in %APPDATA%\Stenwatch, apart from the program, so updating or uninstalling never touches them unless you say so. The feed download is the only traffic leaving the machine.
