Sync and rank, step by step

You press Sync, then Rank. Everything happens inside the one app process: Stenwatch.Core downloads the feeds into cve.db, reads your profile and asset files, matches and scores every finding, and writes the reports. The window shows progress as the engine reports it and refreshes the ranking at the end. The only traffic that leaves the PC is the feed download (and the update check, if you click it).
